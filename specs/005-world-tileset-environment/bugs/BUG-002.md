# Bug Report: Water Bodies Render as Square Cell Blocks Framed by Raw Grass Blocks

**Type**: Spec conflict (BUG-001 shore rules against the art pack's water spec) · Spec gap (no waterline or water-body shape requirement) · Implementation drift (raw reed blocks, per-cell water fill)
**Severity**: High
**Feature**: 005-world-tileset-environment (branch `005-world-tileset-environment`)
**Reported**: 2026-10-05
**Status**: Implemented (viewing checks T050–T052 still open)
**Patched**: 2026-10-05
**Implemented**: 2026-10-05

## Description

The user reports that the world still does not render ponds and lakes. The fix must follow the patterns in the art pack's examples (`zrpg-art/examples/WATER-SPEC.md`, `ponds.md`, `lakes.md`, reference `water.js`). The world must contain **one medium-size lake and a few small ponds**. **The water must not be animated.**

Observed in the running app (headless screenshot at default zoom, `world/world.json`):

1. **The pond is a 96 × 96 px square.** `pond-north` is an ellipse of radius 1.5 cells. It rasterises to 3 × 3 logic cells and is filled cell by cell, so it is drawn as one blue square.
2. **The lake is a staircase of 32 px squares.** `lake-east` is a 7-point polygon (69 cells). Each cell is filled with `fillRect`, so every edge is a 90° step.
3. **The shore is a set of rectangular grass blocks.** `beach-plant-001` (191 × 127, `tall_grass_wide`) and `beach-plant-003` (96 × 127, `tall_grass_block`) are placed raw on every third border cell and turned 90°, 180° or 270°. They read as large square patches of tall grass standing on the grass, not as a shoreline. They do not straddle the waterline, so the hard water–grass edge stays fully visible.
4. **Nothing reads as a pond or a lake.** No waterline, bank face, shallows or rim is drawn. A viewer sees blue squares, not natural water.

Measured: the shore sprites cover only 4.2 % of the water pixels, so the water is not hidden. It is drawn, but in a form that does not read as water.

## Artifact Traceability

### spec.md
- **Affected user story**: Story 2 — Water Bodies with Rocky Shores (all four acceptance scenarios). Story 1 scenario 1 (art from the tileset) and Story 5 scenario 2 (outline change shows in water and shore) are affected indirectly.
- **Affected requirements**:
  - **FR-005**: "Water MUST be drawn with a single static frame… No visible seam". This is kept. The static frame is right. It is missing a rule for how the frame is clipped (to a smooth mask, not to logic cells).
  - **FR-011**: "at least two water bodies of different shape and size, at least one … not a rectangle". The 3 × 3-cell pond meets the words but is still drawn as a square. The requirement has no size or count for ponds and lakes. The user now asks for **1 medium lake + a few (2–3) small ponds**.
  - **FR-012** (rewritten by BUG-001): it requires `beach-plant-001` and `beach-plant-003` **rotated where needed** as transition plants. This **conflicts** with WATER-SPEC §0 and §6: reed sprites are rectangular blocks that must be clipped into a reed-bed shape (§6.3) and never placed raw. "Shore sprites rotated or mirrored to make corners" is listed as an anti-pattern. "Do not mirror sprites": the shadows are baked in.
  - **FR-025** (BUG-001): allows overlays rotated by 90°, 180° or 270°. Its only use is the shore, and WATER-SPEC forbids that use.
  - **FR-024** (BUG-001): "A terrain sprite is a ground-level tile, drawn in the ground layer on the 32 px cell grid". WATER-SPEC draws terrain textures (`rocks2-terrain-020`, `beach-terrain-007` with hole fill) as world-aligned patterns through a distance-based band mask, and places ground decals centred on a point. Neither fits a terrain tile on the cell grid.
  - **FR-016**: water must keep its effect on flies. The logic water mask must be derived from the same rendered shape (WATER-SPEC §7: `S < -3` blocks), or flies will walk on drawn water or stop on drawn grass.
  - **SC-004**: "0 gaps where bare grass touches water with no shore rock, pebble or transition plant". This measures sprite coverage per border cell. WATER-SPEC hides the seam with a continuous shore band plus decor straddling the waterline every 60–100 px. The criterion needs rewording to match.
  - **SC-006**: "no scenery or water changes position or appearance" over 30 s. This is kept. It now explicitly rules out WATER-SPEC's frame animation, flow drift and foam phases.
  - **Edge case "Tiny water bodies"** (< 2 × 2 cells rejected): too small for the WATER-SPEC minimum. A pond needs `rx` ≥ about 50–60 px, or shore bands and decor cover the water (ponds.md).
  - **Assumption "Shoreline with sand and rock"**: describes rock art "along the border". This is replaced by the WATER-SPEC grass (or rocky) shore style.
- **Gap identified**: The spec never says *how* a water body is shaped and edged. It assumes water is a set of logic cells with sprites around it. The atlas has **no** water edge or corner tiles (WATER-SPEC §0.1), so this cannot look natural. The spec needs requirements for: a smooth, noise-roughened waterline from a signed-distance field; a shore band of varying width; a bank face on north banks and a rim on south banks; shallow tint steps; decor that straddles the waterline; and water sizes from ponds.md and lakes.md. It also needs an explicit freeze of WATER-SPEC's animated parts (water frames, drift, foam phases).

## Root Cause Analysis

1. **Spec gap from the start.** The spec treated water as logic cells and shore as sprites placed per border cell. The atlas has no water edge or corner autotiles. The art pack's own guidance says that tiling water cell by cell "always produces square, staircase edges" (WATER-SPEC §0.1). Any per-cell approach, with sand rings (original R1) or with plants (BUG-001), produces the same squares.
2. **BUG-001 patched the symptom into a conflict.** BUG-001 replaced sand with rotated `beach-plant-001` / `beach-plant-003`. Those are tall rectangular reed and grass blocks. WATER-SPEC requires clipping them into reed-bed shapes and forbids rotating or mirroring shore sprites. The patch made the shore look less natural and wrote the anti-pattern into FR-012 and FR-025.
3. **Implementation drift in the fill.** T025 says "clipped to the outline", but `compose.js` fills whole 32 px logic cells. Even the polygon lake loses its outline.
4. **Undersized body.** `pond-north` (rx 1.5 cells = 48 px) is below the pond minimum (`rx` ≥ 60 px). At that size, even a correct pipeline would be all shore.
