# Bug Report: Flowers and Dangers Cluster in a Few Spots; Campfire Logs Must Not Be a Danger

**Type**: Spec gap (no rule for how edibles and dangers are spread) · Spec change (FR-009 names `fire` / `jungle-prop-002` as a required danger)
**Severity**: Medium
**Feature**: 005-world-tileset-environment (branch `005-world-tileset-environment`)
**Reported**: 2026-10-05
**Status**: Implemented (viewing checks T050–T052 still open)
**Patched**: 2026-10-05
**Implemented**: 2026-10-05

## Description

The user asks for two changes:

1. **Spread the flowers and danger objects around the world.** Today they sit in a few spots,
   and large parts of the map have none.
2. **Remove `jungle-prop-002` (`campfire_logs`) as a danger object.**

Measured in `public/world/world.json` (48 × 32 cells). `world-connectome.json` has the same lists.
The map is split into six 16 × 16-cell regions (3 columns × 2 rows):

| Region | Flowers (edible) | Dangers |
|---|---|---|
| North-west (x 0–16, y 0–16) | 0 | 1 spider (3.5, 6.5) |
| North-centre (x 16–32, y 0–16) | 2 (18.5, 11.5), (21.5, 14.5) | 0 |
| North-east (x 32–48, y 0–16) | 1 (36.5, 14.5) | 1 spider (33.5, 15.5), 1 lantern (44.5, 12.5) |
| South-west (x 0–16, y 16–32) | 1 (6.5, 22.5) | 1 fire (12.5, 27.5) |
| South-centre (x 16–32, y 16–32) | 2 (23.5, 17.5), (30.5, 20.5) | 1 fire (26.5, 25.5) |
| South-east (x 32–48, y 16–32) | 0 | 0 |

- 4 of the 6 flowers sit in one 13 × 10-cell patch in the middle (x 18.5–30.5, y 11.5–20.5).
  Two of them are 3.6 cells apart, so their stimulus discs (radius 3) overlap.
- Two regions have no flowers. One region has nothing at all.
- The decorative `meadow-flowers` scatter (ellipse cx 20, cy 16, rx 9, ry 7) uses the same
  sprites as the edible flower (`trees-plant-001` to `trees-plant-003`). It sits on top of that
  same central patch. So the flowers a viewer sees are even more concentrated in the middle.
- Both `fire` entries use `jungle-prop-002`. Taking them out leaves 3 dangers: 2 spiders and
  1 lantern, all in the north half. The whole south half then has no danger. So the removal
  and the spreading must be done together.

## Root Cause Analysis

- **Spread**: This is a spec oversight. The spec and T043 only set minimum counts per kind.
  When the map was laid out again for water (T071), the spots were moved only off the new
  water. Nobody checked where they ended up. Most flowers were put inside the decorative
  meadow, where they look natural, so the middle got them all.
- **Fire**: This is a changed requirement. `campfire_logs` was named in the original request
  and in research R3. The user now rejects it as a danger object.

## Open Decision (for `/speckit.bugfix.patch`)

**Resolved in the patch (2026-10-05)**: option (a), `fire` dropped. The proposed spread rule became FR-032 and SC-013.

- **Fire kind**: (a) drop `fire`, so the dangers are `spider` and `lantern` only
  (recommended). The catalogue has no other fire, torch or flame sprite: only
  `jungle-prop-002` matches. Or (b) keep the `fire` kind with other art the user picks.
- **Spread rule** (proposed): with the map split into 3 × 2 regions of 16 × 16 cells, every
  region has at least one flower and at least one danger. No two flowers are closer than
  6 cells (twice the stimulus radius). There are at least 6 flowers and at least 5 dangers.
  All of them stay off water and the shore band (FR-015).
