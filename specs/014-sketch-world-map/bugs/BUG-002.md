# Bug Report: The World Does Not Equal the Art Pack's Reference World Map

**Type**: Design defect (tile-built map) + asset drift (art pack regenerated)
**Severity**: High
**Feature**: 014-sketch-world-map (branch `016-sketch-world-map`)
**Reported**: 2026-10-15
**Status**: Patched
**Patched**: 2026-10-15

## Description

The shipped map (`example-actual.png`) does not satisfy the requirement that the world be the sketch as the art
pack draws it. The reference reading of `world_map.jpg` is `zrpg-art/examples/world.html` + `world.js`
(`WORLD-SPEC.md`, rendered as `example-expected.png`). The app instead rebuilt the map from cell outlines with
its own tile ground, tile-sized patches and oversized trees, so terrain, forest and water all differ:

1. **Water**: lakes and rivers were cell-unit outlines, not the reference's bodies (Lake Joy with its outlet
   bay, the north river from above the canvas, the Joy → swamp channel, eight swamp pools, Lake Byte with its
   west lobe and island, the south river leaving below the canvas).
2. **Terrain**: no organic ground areas (swamp, sand beach, rocky desert), no trampled-grass trails, no
   mountains; ground was 32 px base tiles with patches. BUG-001 had also removed the dunes and the rocky desert
   (FR-025), which the reference draws.
3. **Forest**: the edge forest was rectangular `edgeForest` bands covered by large-tree scatter, not the
   reference's ragged, jittered border band of small trees with river gaps, plus its groves.
4. **Assets**: the art pack was regenerated (zrpg-art `5d151a3`, catalogue of 2026-10-14). 80 sprite ids moved
   and 22 composites became 9-slice tilesets, so the app's copy of the atlas was stale.

The user's instruction: adopt the new assets and the example world — **terrains, forest and water MUST equal
the example** — and inherit from the app the **world size** (64 × 40 cells of 32 px) and the **allocation of
food and danger and their parameters**.

## Root Cause

The map was authored in this app's tile vocabulary (`ground`, `waterBodies`, `groves`, `scatter` in cells)
and drawn by `compose.js`, a different renderer from the reference. A faithful map needs the reference's
data *and* its renderer (organic area masks, road distance fields, the border band, depth-sorted sprites,
the finish), not a re-authoring of its shapes in cells.

## Fix

- **Assets**: `public/assets/atlas/{catalog.json, atlas-0.png, OBJECTS.md}` replaced by zrpg-art `5d151a3`.
  The tile path's ids (`compose.js`, `ground.js`, `shore.js`) were remapped by source rectangle. The two lost
  composites (cobble, gravel pit) are replaced by the closest single pieces (`cobble_tile_hole`,
  `gravel_splat`).
- **Format**: a world may declare a `map` section instead of the tile sections (contract
  [world-format-sketch-map.md §7](../contracts/world-format-sketch-map.md)). The format stays version 3, and a
  world without `map` is read exactly as before.
- **Renderer**: `world/map-water.js` and `world/sketch-map.js` are a pure port of the reference's `water.js`
  and `world.js` (plan, no DOM); `world/sketch-paint.js` paints the plan. Reference px scale to the world grid
  per axis (sx = 2048/1376, sy = 1280/768) and lengths by √(sx·sy); sprite-scale distances stay in px.
- **Logic**: water = cells whose centre is drawn water; blocked = water + foot cells of solid map sprites
  (tree, bush, rock, prop); edge forest = cells whose centre lies in the border band (danger 100/50/25 %).
- **Worlds**: all seven 64 × 40 worlds carry the same `map`. Their edibles, flies, brains and experiments are
  unchanged (`world-forager-wide-bad.json` had a stale copy of one edible; it now matches its shipped world).
  The two 48 × 32 `-bad` fixtures stay tile worlds.

## Finding: FR-011's zero-heat crossing is impossible with the reference water

The reference river runs from the north edge to the south edge, and its swamp is a chain of pools between the
lakes. No land cell in the swamp is three tiles from water, so **no zero-heat crossing exists**. The best
walkable crossing (4-connected, outside the edge forest) passes between the pools at **heat ≤ 50 %** (one tile
from water). FR-011 is amended to that finding (`tests/water-corridor.test.mjs`, `MAX_CROSSING_HEAT = 0.5`).
Restoring a zero-heat crossing would need different water, which the user's instruction rules out. That is
the user's call, not a tuning step.
