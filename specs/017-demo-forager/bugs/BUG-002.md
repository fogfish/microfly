# Bug Report: a fly at a flower eats for one tick, or not at all

**Type**: Implementation defect + spec gap (ADR 010 D30)
**Severity**: High (R1: a hungry fly must feed and recover)
**Feature**: Demo forager (`017-demo-forager`, ADR 010)
**Reported**: 2026-10-17
**Status**: Patched
**Patched**: 2026-10-17

## Description

After BUG-001, one hungry fly eats when it walks over a flower, but only for a tick at a time. Other flies stall
at a flower, touching it, and do not eat. Requested: a fly eats while it stays in the flower's 100 % odour area.
Asked: should the fly slow below the eating threshold after some energy level?

## Root causes

| # | Cause | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A | Taste and eating used only the flower's centre cell. The sprite and the 100 % odour core (3×3 cells) are larger, so a fly touching the flower off-centre tasted nothing and could not eat | `contactAt`/`stockAt` keyed on `isStimulusCell` of the fly's own cell |
| B | `feed` came from MN9, which barely follows taste in the 14.5K cut: decoded drive 0.032 off food, 0.051 starving on food, at τ = 3 or τ = 20. Eating flickered for single ticks | Eat probe, calibration.md |

## Fix

| # | Change | Files |
|---|---|---|
| A | `flies.food.reach`: the eating area is every cell within Chebyshev distance `reach` of a flower. Taste, eating and the find metric of every arm read it through one helper. Demo worlds: 1 | `fly-world.js` (`flowerAt`), `forager-step-wide.js`, `body-layer.js`, `forager-arms.mjs`, `validate.js`, `fly-config.js`, both demo worlds, `contracts/world-config-v3.md` |
| B | ADR 010 Amendment A2: `feed` reads `drive.taste-mouth` when it exists (MN9 is the fallback). `feedThreshold` 0.12 from the eat probe | `decoder.js`, both demo worlds, ADR 010, `contracts/decoder-v1.md` |

**On slowing down by energy:** not added. The body layer must not read energy (D30, Principle VI). It is not
needed either: a hungry fly in the eating area feeds, so it stands still on 76–90 % of ticks; a sated one feeds on
10 % and walks on. Hunger reaches that decision through the brain (D32's taste gain).

Tests: `tests/demo-forager-bug001.test.mjs` (BUG-002 section).

## Result (scratch diagnostic, 6 flies × 3,000 ticks, start energy 0.3)

Every fly that reaches a flower now feeds and recovers: on the sketch map 0.3 → 1.0, and 0 → 0.5 for two flies
that arrived starving. Flies that never reach a flower still starve; approach is the open locomotion finding
(BUG-001, "Not fixed").

## Not fixed (finding)

A fly near the forest edge paces one line back and forth: each escape reverses its heading by 180° (W14, no
danger side pool), and `wander` 0.4 rad/s is too small to leave the line. Same root as the weak klinotaxis.
