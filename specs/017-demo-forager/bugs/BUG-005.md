# Bug Report: the fly turns 180° often, and not always because of danger

**Type**: Behaviour (escape trigger and direction)
**Severity**: Medium
**Feature**: Demo forager (`017-demo-forager`, ADR 010)
**Reported**: 2026-10-17
**Status**: Patched (heat avoidance trade-off recorded)
**Patched**: 2026-10-17

## Description

The fly turns 180°. Is this the escape signal?

## Root cause (measured, 6 flies × 3,000 ticks per map)

- Every turn of ≥ 150° within 20 ticks coincided with an escape trigger: 155 / 155 (sketch), 167 / 167 (tilemap).
- Every escape reverses: `escapeHeading` returns `heading + π` when the threat's side is unknown, and it always is
  (no danger side pool; heat has `side.heat.R` only).
- 77 of 155 (sketch) and 40 of 167 (tilemap) escapes fired with no danger or heat input: whole-brain bursts lift the
  integrated alarm over 0.18.

## Fix (ADR 010 Amendment A5)

1. `escape.threshold` 0.22 in both demo worlds.
2. `flies.body.escape.turn: { min, max }`: with the side unknown, turn by a random angle in the range, either way.
   Demo worlds: 90°–135° (1.571–2.356 rad).

Files: `body-layer.js`, `validate.js`, both demo worlds, `contracts/world-config-v3.md`, ADR 010. Tests:
`tests/demo-forager-bug001.test.mjs` (BUG-005 section).

## Trade-offs (recorded)

- Food finding beats the random walk on both maps for the first time with the shipped decoder.
- Heat avoidance is lost (worse than the random walk); no turn range tried recovers it without a heat side pool.
- K4 falls: fewer and smaller escapes, and flies roam more, so they meet more danger rings.
