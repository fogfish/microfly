# Bug Report: odour L/R barely differs; flies sit at the water edge; heat does not act like danger

**Type**: Spec gap (ADR 010 Part L contrast gain not implemented; W14 heat) + world physics
**Severity**: Medium
**Feature**: Demo forager (`017-demo-forager`, ADR 010)
**Reported**: 2026-10-17
**Status**: Patched (steering limitation recorded, not fixed)
**Patched**: 2026-10-17

## Description

1. The odour level hardly differs between the antennae whatever the heading, because the fly is small. It reads as
   if the target is always ahead. An artificial filter is wanted on the odour signal.
2. Flies prefer to sit at the water (heat edge). A fly should be able to fly over water, and heat should act like
   danger.

## Root causes

| # | Cause | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A | The antennae sit 1 tile apart and the fields step once per tile, read per cell, so both antennae often sample one cell | L = R on 41 % of ticks for one fly; median \|L − R\| 0.05–0.09 otherwise |
| B | ADR 010 Part L's "declared stimulus contrast gain" was never implemented | — |
| C | Water is in `blocked`: a fly walking into a lake stalls at its edge, and touch fires there | `layout.js` copies water into `blocked` |
| D | A1 removed heat from the alarm read because `drive.heat`'s τ = 3 tail is network-burst noise | correlation 0.87 between heat drive and whole-brain rate at rest |

## Fix

| # | Change | Files |
|---|---|---|
| A, B | `flies.stimuli[sense].interpolate` (bilinear field sampling) and `.contrast { common, gain }` (bilateral filter); demo food odour: interpolate, common 0.5, gain 5; danger and heat: interpolate | `stimulus.js`, `forager-step-wide.js`, `validate.js`, both demo worlds, `contracts/world-config-v3.md` |
| C | `flies.body.crossWater` (v3): water walkable, solid objects not; spawn on dry land | `fly-world.js`, `fly-config.js`, `validate.js`, both demo worlds |
| D | ADR 010 Amendment A3: alarm = max(danger drive, heat drive); `escape.tau` integrates it (demo: 20 ticks, threshold 0.18) | `decoder.js`, `body-layer.js`, `validate.js`, both demo worlds, ADR 010, both contracts |

Tests: `tests/demo-forager-bug001.test.mjs` (BUG-003 section).

## Result (calibration seeds 6–8, 1,500 ticks, shipped decoder)

| Map | Find: brain / random walk | Ticks in danger ring | Ticks in heat ring |
|---|---|---|---|
| sketch | 38.9 % / 55.6 % | 46.8 % / 62.0 % | 6.3 % / 15.0 % |
| tilemap | 22.2 % / 38.9 % | 27.7 % / 66.2 % | 2.5 % / 7.4 % |

Heat and danger are avoided against the random walk on both maps.

## Not fixed (decision recorded)

The filter gives the brain a clear L/R difference, but the decoder has no `side.odour-food` pool: the brain has 3 left-
and 4 right-selective food neurons, under D27's minimum of 5 a side. Lowering it to 3 was measured (find 66.7 % vs
55.6 % and 61.1 % vs 38.9 % for the random walk; hungry − sated +22 % and +44 %) and declined by the user on
2026-10-17: D27 keeps 5. Food approach therefore stays below the random walk.
