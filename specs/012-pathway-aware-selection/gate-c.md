## Reading — this is a significant regression, recorded as a finding (not tuned away)

**The forager brain's held-out foraging ability has collapsed.** Before this feature, v1 found flowers in 42.2 %
of trials (`specs/008-hungry-forager-brain/gate-c.md`), beating the random-walk baseline's 27.2 %. With the
`taste-forward-brake` rule as shipped, v1 finds flowers in only 0.6 % of trials — *below* both the random walk
(23.3 %) and even the size-matched random-graph null (`random-matched`, 11.1 %, which itself improved relative to
its own prior 3.9 %). The structured brain is now worse than unstructured chance at this task.

**Root cause, traced through the two earlier gates in this feature:**

1. Gate A found the rule displaces 703 of 1,500 taste-budget interneurons on the real dataset — a far larger
   effect than the hand-crafted synthetic fixture (which swaps exactly one body by construction). `forward`'s own
   inflow jumped from 206 to 638 edges; `feed`'s collapsed from 193 to 19.
2. A direct LIF probe (see the completion report) found `forward` and `feed` both produce **zero** spike rate at
   the world's existing `outputScale` values ({forward: 32, feed: 8}) — `feed` needs `outputScale` on the order of
   1,000 before it produces any signal at all with this new topology, two orders of magnitude above what is
   configured.
3. With `forward` and `feed` effectively silent, the fly has no measurable drive signal for two of its five
   output channels, which plausibly starves the foraging behaviour Gate C measures — a fly that cannot drive
   `forward` reliably cannot approach a flower at all, consistent with 0 of 118 approach attempts being included.

**This is a calibration mismatch, not a logic bug.** `pathwayBias` does exactly what the contract specifies
(FR-003/FR-004/FR-006 hold, confirmed by both the fixture-level tests and Gate A's `ruleExplained` sanity check:
every displaced-in body has a strictly positive, correctly-seeded backward flow). The regression is that the
*rest of the pipeline* — `world/world-forager.json`'s `outputScale`, calibrated in `008-hungry-forager-brain` for
the *previous* admitted-neuron topology — was never re-tuned for the topology this rule produces. Per
`AGENTS.md`'s "behaviour failures are findings" rule and this session's own direction (recalibration was
explicitly deferred as out of scope for this implementation pass), the rule ships as specified and this collapse
is recorded here, unresolved, for the next calibration pass to address — most likely by raising `feed`'s and
`forward`'s `outputScale` for the new topology (the same kind of pass `008-hungry-forager-brain/calibration.md`
already went through once).

**This number should gate shipping.** Unlike the `eat` metric's prior, milder "short bout" finding (008's own
Gate C), this is not a nuanced miss — v1 is measurably worse than chance at its primary task. Recommend treating
this brain as **not ready to replace the currently-deployed one** until `outputScale` (or another calibration
lever) is re-tuned and Gate C is re-run.
